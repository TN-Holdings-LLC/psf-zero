## 1. What c64 is (`c64/make_c64.py`, `c64/c64_edits.py`)

`make_c64.py` reads c63a and does five things:

1. It applies the exact substitutions and whole-definition replacements in `c64_edits.py`. Each one fixes a switch at
   c63a's default and removes what that value cannot reach.
2. It follows the name references from the roots and removes every top-level definition, variable and import that
   is not reached. Comment blocks directly above a removed statement go with it, and so do section headers whose
   section has become empty.
3. It moves the module docstring, as written, to `CHANGELOG_c63a.md`.
4. It writes a short docstring.
5. It sets `VERSION = "2026-10-16.c64"`.

Running it again gives the same bytes (D2). Nothing was edited by hand.
