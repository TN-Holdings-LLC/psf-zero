## 1. Item 99 (`make_c60.py`, exact substitutions on c59)

**What it covers.** An in-scope input (item 96) that neither item 87's chain formula nor item 91's graph formula
reads. c59 refused these: CZ / CX chains and lattices, `real_amplitudes`, `efficient_su2`, CZ-ring classifiers.
`_native_brickwork` builds them as written, in one fixed pass. Nothing is compared or simulated.

1. **Placement:** item 91's `_graph_place`, i.e. the first VF2 embedding, on the couplers of the device's most common
   two-qubit gate length first, then on all of item 87's cut device. Qubits without a two-qubit gate take the free
   qubits in order.
2. **Order:**
   - Each gate gets a level: 1 + the largest level of an earlier gate on a shared qubit that does not commute with
     it. Diagonal gates commute with each other; nothing else is assumed to commute.
   - Within a level, two-qubit gates come first, by the colour of item 91's edge colouring, then single-qubit gates.
   - "Diagonal" is decided by the gate's name for gates with a parameter (rz, p, cz, cp, crz, rzz, ...), and by the
     matrix only for gates without one. So the class never depends on an angle's value; item 100 relies on this.
   - A CZ chain therefore comes out in two layers instead of a staircase.
3. **Native forms** (each multiplied out and checked to 1e-9):
   - cz stays cz.
   - A diagonal gate is read as Rz x Rz times RZZ(theta), its angles taken from its four phases.
   - RZZ(theta) is first reduced modulo pi (RZZ(pi) is local):
     - 0: no gate;
     - |theta| = pi/2: one cz;
     - with rzz in the Target: rzz(|theta|) in IBM's range, with an X on both sides when theta < 0;
     - otherwise: cz rz cz with H.
   - A written rzz on a Target with rzz is always the native rzz.
   - A single interaction (Weyl b = c = 0: cx, rxx, ryy, crx, ...) is local gates around RZZ.
   - Any other gate (iswap, swap, ...) uses Qiskit's closed-form CZ decomposition (KAK).
4. **Single-qubit runs** are merged and emitted in closed form (ZYZ), by PSF-Zero itself (a20's `_t_zyz` way): one
   rz when diagonal, rz sx rz at theta = pi/2, else rz sx rz sx rz, or rz rx rz with rx in the Target.
5. **Measurements** stay as written. Item 89 is not applied.

**Other changes.**

- The output carries a TranspileLayout.
- `NATIVE_BRICKWORK = False` restores c59. Counts are in `NATIVE99_STATS`.
- The refusal message now names the three formulas.

**Tests.** `test_99.py`: 6 passed. They cover:
