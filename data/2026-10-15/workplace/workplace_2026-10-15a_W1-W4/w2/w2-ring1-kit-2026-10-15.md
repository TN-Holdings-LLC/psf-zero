**The design.**

- Model: DEPTH1's / W3's classifier with breast-cancer data.
  - n = 4 and 6, L = 4 and 8 layers.
  - Weights trained noiselessly (Adam, 300 steps; split seed 1, initial seed 1 + 1000 L) and frozen in JSON, so home needs no training and no
    scikit-learn.
  - 24 test points per configuration (12 per class).
- Arms:
  - A18: a18's `compile_for_model_circuit` with the device's Target and no fractional gates;
  - QK3: Qiskit level 3 with seed 0.
- Control: ghz_n6, compiled once by Qiskit level 3 and submitted twice.
- One job. 800 shots per circuit per repeat, two repeats; the second repeat runs in the reverse order. Dynamical
  decoupling and twirling are off.
- 388 circuits in all, 310,400 shots.
- Before submission, every circuit is checked:
  - native to the device (ISA);
  - free of failed elements;
  - exact within 1e-6.

  The ideal z must also match the model file. A pair is submitted only whole.
- The script refuses to run unless these files match its fixed SHA-256: the release's `psf_compile.py` and
  `benchmarks/psf_ai_compile.py`, a15, a14, TIME1's script and HW2's script.

## 4. Points for home's decision (also in the draft)

**The readout qubit.** The margin is read on the one physical qubit that holds logical qubit 0, and that qubit
differs between the arms. In the rehearsal it was 87 (A18) against 117 and 126 (QK3) for n = 4, and 86 against 144 for
n = 6. In n = 4, L = 8, A18's F was higher but its margin lower. The score table therefore lists the readout qubit. F
(RC) uses every qubit.

**RA is the pooled ratio alone.** Within one n, each arm uses the same placement in every configuration. So "higher
in 3 of 4 configurations" would count two placements twice. The count is reported as RA2.

**RB is weak.** Only 2 points per configuration lie near the boundary (|ideal z| < 0.2). It is in effect a test of
"no loss of accuracy".

**The optional fractional-gate arm** was left out, to stay under 150 s.
