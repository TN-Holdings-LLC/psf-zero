## 1. What item 101 does (`make_c62.py`, exact substitutions on c60)

In c60, items 87/91's CZ path (a Target without rzz and rx) ended with Qiskit's level-3 preset on the fixed layout.
This was the last thing handed to Qiskit inside the scope. In c62, `_cz101_emit` emits it. The form is chosen by block
kind; nothing is compared or searched:

- **One two-qubit term on a pair** (a single interaction: Weyl b = c = 0; ZZ-type, Ising) is a local Clifford around
  RZZ, in closed form (`_n99_zz`). This is the emission c61's template used.
- **Several terms on one pair**, with the single-qubit operations between them (Heisenberg XX + YY + ZZ and others),
  use Qiskit's Weyl decomposition with closed-form circuits (`_t100_block_steps`). Each form is multiplied out and
  checked to 1e-9. This is the form that matched c60 in C61-VAL's probe.
- **Measurements:** diagonal operations directly before them are left out, repeatedly.
- **Single-qubit runs** are merged in closed form (ZYZ). A run with theta = pi becomes one x when x is in the Target.
- **Unchanged:** placement (item 91's first VF2 match), term order and the frame path (F: item 88) are items 87/91's.
- **Switch:** `CZ_OWN_EMIT = False` restores c60. Counts are kept in `CZ101_STATS`.
