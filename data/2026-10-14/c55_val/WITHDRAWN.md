# C55-VAL: withdrawn unrun (2026-10-14)

The owner declared PSF-Zero's scope on 2026-10-14 (Addendum 488): it competes only where it builds by formula, and
hands every other circuit to Qiskit level 3. Item 94, the main change C55-VAL was to test, aimed at 3-regular QAOA
outside that scope and compiled such inputs twice. It is dropped, and C55-VAL is withdrawn before any complete run.

- The prestatement (`C55-VAL_preregistration.md`, SHA-256 in `PREREG_SHA256.txt`) is kept unchanged.
- The two interrupted runs (`run1_interrupted/`, `run2_interrupted/`; 169 and 82 of 700 rows) are kept unchanged and
  were never scored. No verdict is drawn from them.
- Item 95 (item 93's check up to 20 active qubits) is kept and will be validated with the next prototype.
