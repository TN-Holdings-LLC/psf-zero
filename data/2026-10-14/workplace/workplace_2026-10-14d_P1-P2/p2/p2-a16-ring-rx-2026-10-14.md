# P2 (2026-10-14): AI front end a16 -- N2's ring formula as a candidate, single-qubit runs as one rx pulse

**Status: prototype with tests; offline only.** Workplace, 2026-10-14 (times from `TZ=Europe/Zurich date`). It follows
「次の指示2」 P2.

a16 is a15 plus three changes:

- item 28, the ring formula as a candidate;
- item 29, rx as one pulse;
- an executability guard on every candidate.

psf_compile is c51 + items 87-88 (P1), from which a16 uses item 87's placement.

## 1. What a16 adds to a15

**Item 28: the ring formula as a candidate.**

- **Which inputs it reads (`_ring_read`).** The input must be layers, each made of:
  - single-qubit gates; then
  - CZ gates forming one cycle through all its n ≥ 4 qubits.

  It is the same cycle every layer, and a layer's CZs may come in any order. Single-qubit gates and measurements may
  follow the last layer.
- **What it builds.** N2's construction:
  - folded order on a line;
  - rounds of CZ, with interior odd-even SWAPs between rounds;
  - the next layer mirrored.
- **Placement.** The line is placed by psf_compile's item 87 rule: cut, snake piece, else the first VF2 path, with the
  common gate length first. No score is used.
- **Translation.** Qiskit level 3 translates on the fixed layout without routing, given the coupling map and basis
  only. The circuit's wires are bound to line positions, so the output's layout maps the input's qubits correctly.
- **When it is not built.**
  - An unmeasured input with an odd number of layers is not built, because its qubits end permuted (counted).
  - Without item 87 in psf_compile, the candidate is skipped (counted).
- **Order among candidates.** The candidate is added after a15's constructions and before a14's search, which stays
  last (item 27).

**Item 29: rx as one pulse.** With rx in the Target, every candidate's single-qubit runs are merged into rz rx rz
before item 25's comparison. This is exact.

**Item 29: executability guard.** With rzz in the Target, a candidate whose rzz angles fall outside the executable range
is folded by item 86's `_fold`, or dropped if that fails. Without the guard, a15's search candidate on the fractional
FakeTorino returned such circuits (section 4).

Everything else is a15's: choice by `_better` (duration, then two-qubit count), no seed or placement search, failed
elements never used, and the direction backstop.

## 2. Tests (`test_a16.py`, 10 tests, all pass)

- **The ring reader:**
  - a 5-qubit classifier gives cycle [0..4], 3 layers and 5 measurements;
  - shuffled CZ order is accepted;
  - a chain, a different cycle in the second layer, and n = 3 are all rejected.
- **CZ counts:** 7 CZ per layer at n = 4 and 15 at n = 6, exact, chosen as "ring", ISA.
- **Never longer than a15:** for L = 1, 3 and 5.
- **rx on the fractional Target:** no sx in the output, exact, and shorter than on the plain Target.
- **Unmeasured input:**
  - with an even number of layers, the candidate implements the unitary (item 39's `_implements`);
  - with an odd number of layers, it is not built (counted).
- **Switches:** with `RING_FORMULA = False` and `RX_ONE_PULSE = False`, the output is identical to a15's.
- **Deterministic.**
- **A failed qubit on the line is cut first,** and the output is still exact.
- **An rzz angle out of range is folded,** and the result still implements the circuit.

## 3. Results on W3's 64 cells (`eval/`)

**Setup.**

- Cells: FakeFez and FakeTorino; n = 4 and 6; L = 1-16; W3's angles.
- T is the device's own Target. F is the same with rzz and rx added (test_c51's construction).
- Front ends:
  - a15 runs on c49 for T and on c51 for F;
  - a16 runs on c51 + 87-88 for both.
- QK3 is Qiskit level 3 on T. QK3F is Qiskit level 3 on F, folded by FoldRzzAngle.

**Where the ring candidate was not chosen.** The 3 exceptions are all L = 1, the cells N2 already lost:

- FakeFez and FakeTorino at n = 4 chose a14's search (6 CZ against the formula's 7);
- FakeFez at n = 6 chose a15's construction of the input.

On the 61 ring cells, a16 on T equals N2's own rows exactly.

**rx as one pulse (item 29):** a16 on F takes 0.93 of its duration on T (geometric mean; range 0.87-0.96). The

## 4. Side findings for home (a15 on c51 with a fractional Target)

- **a15's search candidate returns rzz angles out of range.** a14's searched output is never folded, and in 2 of 64
  cells (FakeTorino n = 6, L = 7 and 15) a15 returned it with rzz outside 0 < θ ≤ π/2. a16's guard (item 29) folds
  such a candidate.
- **One a15 output is not exact.** FakeFez n = 6, L = 12 has a total variation distance of 1.4e-5 (chosen candidate
  "commuted"). Item 86's re-synthesis keeps a circuit when `_same_action` accepts it within EXACT_TOL, and this one
  passed, so the tolerance may be too loose for item 86. a16's choice in that cell is the ring, which is exact.
- **QK3F's raw output had rzz angles out of range in all 64 cells** before folding.
