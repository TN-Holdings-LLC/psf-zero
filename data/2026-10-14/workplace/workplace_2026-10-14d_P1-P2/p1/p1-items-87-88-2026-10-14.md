# P1 (2026-10-14): items 87 (chain brickwork) and 88 (rzz with a Pauli frame), a workplace proposal on prototype c51

**Status: patch proposal with tests; offline only.** Workplace, 2026-10-14, finished 04:57 CEST (`TZ=Europe/Zurich date`). It follows 「次の指示2」 P1 and 「次の指示3」.

Common rule followed:

- No search, no sweep, no seed variation, and no choice by ESP or simulation.
- The placement takes the first VF2 match, with no score.
- Measured: correctness, two-qubit count (CZ + rzz), and duration (ASAP on the Target's gate lengths).
- Carried X gates are undone inside the circuit. No measured bit is flipped afterwards.

## 2. What items 87 and 88 do

**Item 87 (N1, renumbered from 86)** applies under these conditions:

- the device is given (`steiner` "auto" true);
- no `coupling_map`, `initial_layout`, `final_resynthesis`, `compare_level3` or `compare_floor` is passed;
- the device's two-qubit gate is cz;
- the input's terms act on one or two qubits, and the two-qubit terms form one path.

Construction and placement are unchanged from N1:

- **Cut:** W5's cut of failed elements.
- **Placement:** the snake piece or the first VF2 path, trying couplers of the most common gate length first.
- **Order:** levels, then even edges, then odd edges, then single-qubit terms.

The result is compared with **c51's own output** for the same call (c49, then item 86) by item 84's rule; a tie keeps
the brickwork. The comparison now sits in the outermost wrapper, so c51's own output already includes item 86's
fractional re-synthesis. Nested calls go straight to c51.

**Item 88** applies when the Target has both rzz and rx (`CHAIN_FRAME`):

- **Emission.**
  - Each two-qubit term becomes one rzz between basis changes (X: h; Y: rx(π/2) … rx(−π/2)).
  - Each X term becomes rx.
  - Diagonal gates directly before measurements are dropped (RemoveDiagonalGatesBeforeMeasure, as Qiskit level 3
    did for N1). Home's item 89 applies the same rule on c52.
- **Angles (`_chain_frame`).**
  - Each angle is reduced to (−π, π].
  - If |θ| > π/2, it is moved by π using Z⊗Z: two virtual rz(π) gates. The phase is kept exactly, including the sign
    rz(π) picks up when its qubit is in the frame.
- **Negative angle.**
  - One x is placed before the rzz, on its first qubit; it merges into the single-qubit run before it.
  - The x that would follow the rzz is kept in a Pauli X frame. Later single-qubit gates G are placed as X G X
    (merged into one pulse), and a later rzz with exactly one qubit in the frame has its sign flipped.
- **The frame is undone inside the circuit:**
  - one x before the qubit's measurement; or
  - one x at the end of an unmeasured qubit, merged into the last single-qubit gate where there is one.

  No bit is flipped afterwards, and an unmeasured circuit is the same unitary, global phase included (tested).
- **Placement and single-qubit runs.**
  - Qiskit's level-0 preset places the circuit on the fixed layout (no routing, coupling map and basis only).
  - Every single-qubit run is merged into rz rx rz (`Optimize1qGatesDecomposition(basis=["rz","rx"])`), one pulse.
- **Safety.** If an angle were still out of range, item 86's `_fold` would handle it. This was never seen.

Without rzz or rx in the Target, item 87's output is N1's CZ brickwork. A test checks it is identical to the c49+N1
file.

## 3. Tests

**`test_87_88.py`: 13 tests, all pass.**

- TFIM n = 8 and 12 on FakeFez with rzz and rx (test_c51's `fractional`) have:
  - rzz and rx only (no cz or sx), all rzz angles in range, and ISA;
  - the exact outcome distribution;
  - a duration at or below c51's.
- Unmeasured TFIM, sign-random TFIM, an XYZ chain, and mixed terms (XY, YZ, ZX, XZ) are the same unitary **with the
  global phase**. Item 39's `_implements` also accepts them.
- Sign-random TFIM, measured and unmeasured, is exact; angles above π/2 were folded and the frame was undone at the
  end of unmeasured qubits (counters checked).
- No operation follows a measurement on its qubit, and there is no classical control.
- An XYZ chain and mixed terms are exact.
- One rzz per term: the frame never builds rzz(−θ) from two gates.
- On FakeFez's own Target (no rzz), the output is identical to the c49 + N1 file for TFIM n = 8 and 12 and an XYZ
  chain.
- With `CHAIN_FRAME = False`, item 87 gives the CZ brickwork on the rzz Target.
- A ring input is not handled; the output is identical to c51's.
- The output is deterministic.
- The output is never longer than c51's.
- n = 100 with 10 steps is executable, ISA and under 4 µs.

**c51's own 63 tests on the patched file:** 60 pass. The 3 failures are the three `test_version` tests, which expect
"2026-10-14.c51" (`c51_tests_on_patched_file.txt`).

**Notes.**

- The TFIM duration does not depend on n: 0.47 / 0.85 / 1.62 / 2.00 µs at 2 / 4 / 8 / 10 steps on FakeFez, for n = 12
  to 100.
- **Why the two-qubit count is below c51's:** the last ZZ layer stands directly before the measurements and is
  diagonal, so it is dropped. Without measurements the counts are equal. Home accepted this for N1.
- **Compile time:** at most 25 s (n = 100, including c51's own compile for the comparison).
- **XYZ inputs are listed even edges, then odd edges** (a brickwork product formula). A product formula listed edge
  by edge in the order XX, YY, ZZ is serial by definition: XX(0,1), YY(0,1), XX(1,2) … chain through anticommuting
  pairs. Both item 87 and c51 then reproduce it exactly at the same long duration (20.8 µs at n = 10, 4 steps, in the
  first quick check). That is a property of the input, not a compiler loss.

## 5. Side findings for home

- **QK3F given the Pauli evolution is 8-25 times longer than given the brickwork.** Qiskit's synthesis of the
  PauliEvolutionGate keeps the term order, which is serial. On the classifier circuits, QK3F's raw rzz angles were out
  of range in most cells (column `chosen` = `raw_in_range=False`). Qiskit level 3 does not fold by itself, as C50-VAL
  found.
- **FoldRzzAngle can leave a `global_phase` instruction,** which Aer rejects. c51's `_strip_global_phase_gates`
  handles this; any harness that folds Qiskit's output must strip it too.
