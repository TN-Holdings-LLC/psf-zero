# R2 (2026-10-14): where the ~1e-7 differences come from, and what tightening item 93 to 1e-9 needs

**Status: cause found, fix prototyped; offline only.** Workplace, 2026-10-14, finished 09:11 CEST
(`TZ=Europe/Zurich date`). Base: c54 (bundle g).

## 1. Which outputs are inexact (`r2_scan.py`, `scan_rows.jsonl`)

**Setup.**

- C52-VAL's 24 tests × 5 devices × {T, F} = 240 cells, through c54's default call (C52-VAL's arms' arguments).
- Measure: largest outcome-probability difference, wherever at most 20 qubits are active (194 cells).

**Result.** Only **6 cells exceed 1e-12**, and all are **tfim_n20_r3**:

- 1.54e-8 on T on all five devices;
- 3.2e-7 on F on FakeTorino.

Every other cell is ≤ 5.1e-14.

**Why C54-VAL saw at most 5.0e-14.** tfim_n20_r3 has **20 active qubits**, so item 93 does not check it: its check
stops at RESYNTH_MAX_QUBITS = 16, and these cells are `not_checkable`. The 1e-7 / 1e-8 outputs therefore exist in c54,
outside item 93's view.

**c48's 12-qubit TFIM ring on FakeTorino** (home's 6.1e-7) is within item 93's view. It passes at 1e-6.

## 2. Cause (`r2_probe.py`, `r2_bisect.py`, `r2_bisect2.py`)

Every transpile call inside c54 was wrapped. The error appears in STEINER6's final
`transpile(..., optimization_level=3, routing_method="none")` and nowhere else. Bisecting its passes by callback:

| case | UnitarySynthesis (translation) | TwoQubitPeepholeOptimization | CommutativeCancellation |
|---|---|---|---|
| ring12 FakeTorino | 0 → 1.07e-6 | → 6.1e-7 | (see below) |
| tfim_n20_r3 FakeFez | 0 → 3.25e-8 | → 1.54e-8 | — |

**Source 1 — the Weyl snap (as in Q3).**

- Of the blocks the peephole re-synthesises, 10 of 36 (ring) and 3 of 50 (n = 20) come back with gate infidelity
  3.9e-10 to 8.7e-10.
- Their exact Weyl coordinates are (a, 2e-5, 2e-5) or (a, 0, 0). Qiskit snaps them to (a, 0, 0), a controlled-phase
  form, because the snap costs less than 1e-9 in fidelity.
- The tiny b and c are real: a ZZ rotation and single-qubit X rotations meet in one block. An exact synthesis with the
  **same CZ count** exists (section 3).

**Source 2 — CommutativeCancellation.** With the synthesis made exact, the FakeTorino ring still moved by 3.3e-7, at
CommutativeCancellation (pass 28 of the optimisation loop). Removing that one pass made it exact (1.6e-10) at the same
duration. The mechanism inside the Rust pass (an approximate commutation or merge) was not traced further.

## 3. A synthesis without the snap (`exact2q.py`, prototype)

**`exact_2q` / `_refine`.**

- Qiskit's synthesis chooses the gate count and structure: CZ, or rzz with angles.
- Its single-qubit layers, and any rzz angles, are then solved to equal the block exactly. This is Levenberg-Marquardt,
  started at Qiskit's own values, with the global phase free. If no exact solution exists with that structure, the
  generic three-CZ form is solved instead (never needed here).
- **The Q3 block:** Qiskit's 2 CZ at infidelity 8.5e-10 become **2 CZ at 6.7e-16**.
- **200 random unitaries:** worst infidelity 6.7e-16.

**`exact_level3(**kw)`.** This is Qiskit's level-3 preset with three changes:

- every UnitarySynthesis is replaced by ExactUnitarySynthesis (Qiskit's pass, then `_refine`);
- TwoQubitPeepholeOptimization is replaced by ConsolidateBlocks + ExactUnitarySynthesis (the pre-2.x pair);
- optionally, CommutativeCancellation is removed.

| STEINER6 final transpile | Qiskit level 3 | exact | exact without CommutativeCancellation |
|---|---|---|---|
| ring12 FakeTorino | 6.1e-7, 1.888 µs | 3.3e-7, 2.132 | **1.6e-10, 2.132** |
| tfim_n20_r3 FakeFez | 1.5e-8, 1.320 | **4.6e-14, 1.452** | 4.7e-14, 1.452 |
| Q3's cell (a15) | 2.2e-6, 14.83 | **5.6e-15, 14.81** | 2.7e-15, 14.76 |

**Cost.** Up to 13% in duration (+5 two-qubit gates on the ring). ConsolidateBlocks + UnitarySynthesis decides worse
than the peephole. A peephole that keeps Qiskit's choice per block and only re-solves its single-qubit gates would avoid
this; it is not built yet.

## 4. Item 93 at 1e-9 (`r2_fallback.py`, check extended to 20 active qubits)

| case | 1e-6 (as c54) | 1e-9, c54's fallback | 1e-9 with the exact level 3 as STEINER6's final transpile |
|---|---|---|---|
| tfim_n20_r3 FakeFez T | 1.32 µs, 1.5e-8 | **27.62 µs** (usual path), exact | **1.452 µs, 4.6e-14, no fallback** |
| tfim_n20_r3 FakeMarrakesh T | 1.40, 1.5e-8 | 28.17 (usual path) | **1.540, 2.6e-14, no fallback** |
| c48 ring12 FakeTorino T | 1.824, 6.1e-7 | 23.78 (usual path) | 23.78 (CommutativeCancellation left in; would pass without it) |

**c54's fallback order is very costly.** The usual path is 17-21× longer on these Ising circuits.

**Will all C52-VAL cells pass at 1e-9 once fixed?**

- **With item 93 as it is (≤ 16 qubits):** yes, trivially, because the 6 inexact cells are not checked.
- **With the check extended to 20 qubits:** the 6 cells fail. With c54's fallback they become exact but 17-21× longer.
  With the exact level 3 they pass at +10%, provided CommutativeCancellation is also removed for the FakeTorino F cell.

## 5. Proposal for home (next item number 94 or later; not built into c54 here)

1. **Extend item 93's distribution check to ≤ 20 active qubits.** A statevector of 20 qubits is 16 MB. Otherwise the
   inexact outputs sit outside the check.
2. **Replace STEINER6's final transpile** (and item 80's level-3 candidate, and the usual path's level-3 call) with an
   exact pass manager:
   - exact two-qubit synthesis (`_refine` of Qiskit's own choice);
   - no CommutativeCancellation, or a strict replacement.
   Keep Qiskit's peephole decisions by re-solving its blocks rather than swapping it out, to avoid the 10% cost.
3. **Then set item 93's tolerance to 1e-9,** with the exact pass manager as the first fallback, before the usual path.
4. **Until then,** keep 1e-6, and report that tfim_n20_r3's outputs carry 1.5e-8 to 3.2e-7, unchecked.
