# Workplace design note (written before any HW1 data is looked at with it): candidate psf_ai_compile 2026-10-13.a14 -- an idle-aware, state-aware estimate, duration-diverse candidates, device-aware compiles and a failed-element guarantee for the AI front end (2026-10-13)

**Status: design, fixed before implementation results and before the estimate is evaluated on any hardware data.**
This note is saved to the Project before the estimate is computed on HW1's circuits (Addendum 455), so that its
form cannot have been fitted to them.

## 1. Why

HW1 (Addendum 455): ESP, which charges gate and readout errors and nothing for time, ranked PSF-Zero against Qiskit
level 3 wrongly on the real device (H1, H2 refuted). The AI front end (a12) chooses with a4's state-aware estimate,
which has the same blind spot: thermal relaxation is charged only while a gate runs; a qubit waiting between gates
costs nothing. a12 also (i) scores only candidates within 2 two-qubit gates of the fewest, so a slightly larger but
much shorter circuit is never scored, (ii) compiles its small-circuit candidates without the Target, so none of the
release's device-aware steps (items 58-64) apply there, and (iii) has no final check against failed elements.

## 2. Changelog items

- **19 (device-aware candidates).** With a `target`, every starting point also gets one compile by the release's
  call with that Target (`target=target`, release defaults otherwise, first seed). These candidates are always scored
  (like a7's level-3 output). a12's own candidates are kept, so a14's candidate set contains a12's.
- **20 (idle term).** The estimate adds, for every qubit, the first-order loss of state fidelity while it waits
  (form fixed in section 3). It is added to a4's gate terms and a10's readout terms, in the candidate choice and in
  the re-placement.
- **21 (duration-diverse candidates).** Besides a12's cut (within REMAP_EXTRA_2Q of the fewest two-qubit gates, at
  most REMAP_TOP), the DURATION_EXTRA = 3 candidates with the shortest scheduled duration are also scored.
- **22 (failed-element guarantee).** The chosen circuit must not use an element the Target reports failed (release
  `_failed_elements(target, 0.5)` and `_uses_failed`). If it does, the best-scored candidate that does not is taken;
  if none, the release's recommended call is returned (which raises `FailedElementsError` when nothing avoids them).

Without a `target`, a14 behaves exactly as a12. Above SMALL_MAX_QUBITS nothing changes (the release's recommended
call, a8).

## 3. The idle term (fixed here)

**Schedule.** As soon as possible, on the candidate's physical qubits, with each instruction's duration from the
Target (`target[name][qargs].duration`; `rz`, `barrier` and instructions without a duration take 0; a barrier
synchronises its qubits). A qubit is idle from the end of its previous instruction to the start of its next one,
and after its last instruction until its measurement, or until the end of the circuit if it is not measured. Time
before a qubit's first instruction is charged too, and costs nothing because the qubit is in |0>. Nothing is
charged after a measurement. The schedule is computed once per candidate on its own placement and reused when the
candidate is re-placed (on the devices used, gate durations are uniform per gate type); only T1 and T2 follow the
placement.

**Loss while idle (first order, not Pauli-twirled).** For a qubit whose reduced state at the start of an idle
window of length t has Bloch components (x, y, z), under amplitude damping (rate 1/T1) and pure dephasing (rate
1/T_phi = 1/T2 - 1/(2 T1), T2 clamped to at most 2 T1), the first-order loss of fidelity of the whole (ideal, pure)
state is

  t / T1 * [(1 - z)/2 - (x^2 + y^2)/4]  +  t / T_phi * (1 - z^2)/2

(from the Lindblad rates <L^dag L> - |<L>|^2 with L = sigma_minus / sqrt(T1) and L = Z / sqrt(2 T_phi)). Checks:
|0> gives 0, |1> gives t/T1, |+> gives t/(2 T2). The reduced state of a waiting qubit does not change while other
qubits are acted on, so (x, y, z) are taken from the ideal state right after the qubit's previous instruction.
Summed over windows, each logical qubit q carries A_q = sum t (1 - z)/2 - t (x^2 + y^2)/4 and
B_q = sum t (1 - z^2)/2, and a placement p costs sum_q A_q / T1(p(q)) + B_q (1/T2(p(q)) - 1/(2 T1(p(q)))).
A qubit without T1 is not charged.

**What it assumes.** No dynamical decoupling and no twirling (HW1's settings: both off). With dynamical decoupling
the dephasing term would be smaller; this estimate is not for that setting. It is first order: it ranks
candidates, it does not predict the size of a large loss.

**What is not changed.** a4's gate terms (Pauli-twirled relaxation during the gate plus the depolarising remainder
of the reported error), a10's readout terms, a11's direction rules, a12's caches.

## 5. Implementation note (added 2026-10-13, before any evaluation on hardware data)

The first smoke run showed that a candidate re-placed with the schedule of its unplaced form can be ranked on a
slightly different number than its placed form gives (readout durations differ between qubits). The final choice
therefore re-computes the estimate on each placed candidate (its own schedule); the placement search still reuses the
unplaced schedule as in section 3. No other change.
